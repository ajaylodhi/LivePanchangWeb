The Eye of the Vedas (Drishya-Tulya Precision)

Traditional printed paper Panchangs are often calculated for a single city (like Ujjain or Varanasi) using ancient uncorrected tables. This creates a time discrepancy of 1 to 2 hours in Tithi ending times for other locations.

As stated in classical Jyotish Shastra: "प्रत्यक्षशास्त्रं साचितारुनेत्रम्" (Astronomy is the visible eye of the Vedas). Live Panchang calculates real-time planetary longitudes using our High-Precision Celestial Positioning Model (Chitrapaksha / Lahiri Ayanamsha) accurate to fractions of a second for your exact GPS latitude and longitude.

The Five Limbs of Time (Panchanga)

High-Precision Real-Time Calculations for Every Vedic Element

Pillar 1

1. Tithi (Lunar Day)

Calculated from the 12-degree longitudinal difference between Sun and Moon. 30 Tithis per lunar month divided into Shukla Paksha and Krishna Paksha with exact start and end timestamps.

  • Evaluates Udaya Tithi (Sunrise prevalence)
  • Displays exact transition time to next Tithi
  • Sun & Moon Rashi indicators
Pillar 2

2. Vara (Solar Weekday)

The solar day governed by planetary lords. In Vedic time, Vara begins strictly at local Surya Udaya (Sunrise) and lasts until the next day's Sunrise (Udayat-Udayam).

  • Sunrise to Sunrise Vedic day boundary
  • Determines Day Lord for Horas & Choghadiya
Pillar 3

3. Nakshatra (Lunar Mansion)

The 360-degree zodiac ecliptic path divided into 27 constellations of 13° 20' each. Live Panchang provides the active Nakshatra, its Pada (quarter), and exact transition moment.

  • 27 Nakshatras with Pada details
  • Essential for birth stars & muhurats
Pillar 4 & 5

4 & 5. Yoga & Karana

Yoga is the angular sum of solar and lunar ecliptic longitudes (27 Yogas from Vishkambha to Vaidhriti). Karana is half of a Tithi (6 degrees separation, 60 Karanas per month).

  • Major inauspicious Yogas highlighted
  • Vishti Karana explicitly flagged as Bhadra
